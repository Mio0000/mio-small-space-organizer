Privacy Policy — Mio Small Space Scheduler

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Mio Small Space Scheduler ("the app") is a personal tool operated by Mio | Small Space Organizer (pinterest.com/mio_lifestyle). It is used only to publish and schedule Pins to the operator's own Pinterest account. It is not offered to other users.

What data the app accesses

  • The list of boards on the operator's own Pinterest account, to choose where Pins are published.
  • The Pins the app creates on the operator's own account.

The app does not access other users' Pins, boards, profiles or any personal information about other Pinterest users.

How data is stored

Pinterest access tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer in a local configuration file. They are never shared, sold or sent to any third party other than Pinterest's own API.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Pinterest settings (Security → Apps / Connected apps). Locally stored tokens are deleted when the app is no longer used.

Contact

For questions, contact the operator via the Pinterest profile above.
